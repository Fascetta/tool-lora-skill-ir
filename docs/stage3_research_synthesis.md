## Overall result

Stage 3 is partially successful. On the controlled hidden-ontology protocol task, learner-visible demonstrations can be converted into a canonical, persistent, hybrid Skill IR without supplying the evaluator's semantic ontology for the tested relations. The artifact contains learned relational structure and losslessly preserved opaque lexical identity, survives support deletion, and resolves through an ontology-free structural interface into the independently validated frozen executor.

The result does not establish discovery of the evaluator's modular ontology. Stage 3C found reproducible statistical organization, but not independently manipulable semantic modules or discovered composition.

## Final conclusion

Learner-visible demonstrations were sufficient to construct a canonical persistent hybrid Skill IR and deterministically reconnect it to the validated executor on the controlled task. This is an information-sufficiency and persistence result, not arbitrary latent ontology discovery. The primary negative result is that the tested generic relation representation does not identify fine, independently manipulable factors or support discovered composition.
